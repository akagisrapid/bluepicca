Please try the following.

0. Fix for a bug in 1.2.7
   - Check that you can switch to your saved feeds from "Home" at the top left (in 1.2.7 the list did not appear)

1. Opening profiles
   - Open a profile from an avatar on the timeline and from your own icon at the top right, and check that it slides in from the right
   - Check that swiping from the left edge or tapping Back returns one screen at a time
   - Scroll to the very top and check that the header image extends under the Back button with no gap

2. Profile screen
   - Check that "No replies / With replies / Media" above the posts switches what is shown (No replies is the default)
   - On your own profile, check that the Edit button opens the editor
   - On someone else's profile, choose "…" → "Set background color" and check that the color also appears at the top of their profile
   - Open one of your own posts and check that the trash button sits at the right of the name row and deletes the post

3. Where mute words apply
   - Add a mute word and check that posts are hidden when the word appears only in a quoted post, a link card, or an image description (ALT)
   - Check that they are also hidden in search results, profiles, and thread replies

4. Mute word duration, target and sync
   - In Settings → Mute words, choose "Tags only" and a duration (such as 24 hours), add a word, and check that the list shows "Tags only · Until <date>"
   - Check that a "Tags only" word hides posts with that hashtag but keeps posts that merely contain the same text
   - Check that a mute word added in the official app appears in Bluepicca when you come back to it, and that a word added in Bluepicca appears in the official app

If something looks wrong, please report it along with what you did.
