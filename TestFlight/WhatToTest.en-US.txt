Fixed jittering when swiping down to close the photo viewer.

1. Open a post with photos and tap a photo to view it full screen
2. Swipe down to close it. Check that the photo follows your finger smoothly, even when swiping slightly diagonally
3. Drag down a little and let go. Check that the photo returns to its original position
4. On a post with multiple photos, check that swiping left and right still pages between them

Please also keep trying push notifications.

1. Settings > Notifications: turn on "Receive notifications" and allow notifications
2. Check that "Registered for push notifications (production)" appears under the Notifications section
3. Close the app and have another account reply to one of your posts
4. Check that a "... replied to you" notification arrives within a few seconds

If registration fails, the error is shown in the Notifications section of Settings. Please include it in your report.
