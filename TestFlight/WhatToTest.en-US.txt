Please try the following three things.

1. Edit your own profile
   - Open Settings and tap your card at the top to expand it
   - Change your name and bio, tap Save, and check that the profile screen shows the change
   - Check that you can pick a new avatar and header image from Photos and save them
   - Check that you can set pronouns with a button such as "they/them" or by typing
   - Check that you can add a link (for example yoursite.com with a title), reorder it, delete it, and save
   - Check that the official app or the web shows the same profile

2. Profile screen
   - Open someone's profile and check that their posts are listed right away
   - Scroll down and check that the top part scrolls away while only the Posts / Followers / Following buttons stay at the top
   - Check that the buttons show the post, follower and following counts
   - Check that pronouns appear next to the @handle and links appear as buttons; with many links, check that you can scroll them sideways
   - Check that "Follows you" appears when the person follows you

3. Actions on the profile screen
   - Swipe a post left or right and check that you can like or repost it, just like on the home timeline
   - Check that pull to refresh works and that more posts load when you reach the bottom
   - Check that the "…" menu at the top right lets you mute, hide reposts and block
   - For someone you follow, tap "Following" and check that you are asked before unfollowing

If something looks wrong, please report it along with what you did.
