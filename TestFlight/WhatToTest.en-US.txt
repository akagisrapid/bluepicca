Please try push notifications.

1. Settings > Notifications: turn on "Receive notifications" and allow notifications
2. Check that "Registered for push notifications (production)" appears under the Notifications section
3. Close the app and have another account reply to one of your posts
4. Check that a "... replied to you" notification arrives within a few seconds

If registration fails, the error is shown in the Notifications section of Settings. Please include it in your report.
