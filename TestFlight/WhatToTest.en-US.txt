Please try the following three things.

1. Profiles after leaving the app open for a while
   - Leave the app open for more than 2 hours without closing it, then open your own or another user's profile
   - Check that the name, follower counts, and so on appear (they used to show a blank screen)

2. When a profile fails to load
   - Turn on Airplane Mode and open a profile
   - Check that "Couldn't load the profile" appears and that tapping OK takes you back to the previous screen

3. Swiping in the photo viewer
   - Open a post with photos and tap a photo to view it full screen
   - Swipe down to close it. Check that the photo follows your finger smoothly, even when swiping slightly diagonally
   - Drag down a little and let go. Check that the photo returns to its original position
   - On a post with multiple photos, swipe left and right and check that pages change smoothly. Also check that the photo does not jump when you swipe quickly several times in a row

If anything looks wrong, please include what you did in your report.
